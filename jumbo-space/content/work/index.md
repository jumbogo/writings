## Experience

**Auger**, Platform Senior Software Engineer\
Bellevue, WA · Dec 2025 – present

Built Auger's autonomous order management system, and moved the company's ETL
pipelines from Fabric to Temporal workflows. Serve as the in-house Temporal
expert and Temporal Cloud account owner.

**Temporal Technologies**, Senior Software Engineer, Cloud Platform\
Seattle, WA · 2022 – 2025

Control-plane lead for bringing Temporal Cloud to Google Cloud, from
architecture through general availability in 2025. Designed and shipped
Temporal Cloud's audit logging (GA 2023) and private connectivity rules for
namespaces.

**Bomi AI Technology (Hangzhou)**, Co-founder & Director\
Hangzhou, China · 2019 – 2022

Co-founded a startup building AI-driven fault detection and diagnostics for
building HVAC systems. Led engineering end to end, from edge data collection to
the cloud platform and customer dashboards, and worked directly with customers,
channel partners and investors.

**Google**, Software Engineer & Tech Lead\
Kirkland & Seattle, WA · 2007 – 2019

Twelve years across engineering productivity and Google Cloud: built a
company-wide engineering productivity metrics platform, a pricing simulation
system for Cloud Billing, and the performance and integration test framework
behind the launches of Persistent Disk, Cloud SQL and Compute Engine.
