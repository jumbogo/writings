Hi, I'm Zhengbo, though most people call me Jumbo or ZZ. I'm a software engineer and entrepreneur who has lived in the US for over twenty years. I've worked at companies of every size, from Google to startups like Temporal and Auger, and spent a few years running my own startup in China. I've worked through each wave along the way, from the early internet boom through cloud and big data, and now I'm in the middle of the AI era, exploring how it can create real value for more people.

This site holds my engineering notes in [Tech](/tech/), my writing about running, travel and family (mostly in Chinese) in [Life](/life/), and my experience, patents and publications in [Work](/work/).
