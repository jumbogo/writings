Hi, I'm Zhengbo, though most people call me Jumbo. I'm a software engineer and entrepreneur who has lived in the US for over twenty years. I've worked at companies of every size, from Google to Temporal and Auger, and spent a few years running my own startup in China. I've ridden each wave along the way, from the early internet boom through cloud and big data, and now I'm in the middle of the AI era, exploring how it can create real value for more people.

This site holds my engineering notes in [Tech](/tech/) and, mostly in Chinese, my writing about running, travel and family in [Life](/life/).
